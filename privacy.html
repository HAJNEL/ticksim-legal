Privacy Policy

TickSim Uploader · last updated 28 October 2026

Whose data

The only TikTok account the tool connects to is the TickSim channel's own account. It has no other users and collects no data from viewers or other TikTok users.

What it stores

  • The access and refresh tokens TikTok issues when the account owner approves the tool, plus the account's open ID and display name (to confirm the right account is connected).
  • The publish IDs and status TikTok returns for each video it posts.

These are stored only on the creator's own computer, are never sold or shared, and are used only to post TickSim's videos.

Scopes used

  • user.info.basic: to show which account is connected.
  • video.publish: to post TickSim's videos directly to its profile.
  • video.upload: to send a video to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft when direct posting isn't available.

Deleting data

Revoking the app in TikTok (Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions) stops all access; the stored tokens are then deleted.
