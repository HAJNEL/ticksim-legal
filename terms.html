TickSim Uploader · last updated 28 October 2026

What it is

TickSim Uploader is a private automation run by the creator of the TickSim channel (@ticksim) on their own computer. It publishes TickSim's own videos to TickSim's own YouTube channel and TikTok account, following a schedule the creator controls. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Use of TikTok

The tool uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API only on the TickSim account, and only after that account's owner has signed in and approved it. Every post follows TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions → Manage app permissions).

Use of YouTube

On YouTube the tool uses the YouTube API Services only on the TickSim channel, with that channel owner's approval. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Content

All videos posted are original TickSim content owned by the channel. The tool does not post, collect or modify anyone else's content.
